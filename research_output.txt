Saudi Arabia Vision 2030 is an ambitious economic and social reform plan that was announced in April 2016 by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. 
The plan aims to reduce the country's dependence on oil, diversify its economy, and develop public service sectors such as health, education, infrastructure, recreation, and tourism. 
The vision is built around three primary themes: a vibrant society, a thriving economy, and an ambitious nation. 
Vision 2030 also seeks to create job opportunities for Saudi citizens, foster innovation, and promote social welfare programs. 
The plan includes various initiatives such as the development of the entertainment industry, investments in renewable energy, and the promotion of tourism in historic and cultural sites.
